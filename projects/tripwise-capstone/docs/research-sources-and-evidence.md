# TripWise — Research sources and evidence notes

## Official product sources reviewed on 18 October 2026

1. Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Domestic Tourism Expenditure Survey / Domestic Tourism in India (23 September 2026): https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2313894&lang=1&reg=3
   - Source relevance: trip expenditure is collected across categories including accommodation, food/drink, transport, shopping, recreation, religious, cultural, sporting and health-related activities.
   - Limitation: national survey context does not validate the specific TripWise proto-personas or their pain-point frequency.

2. MakeMyTrip investor press release, “MakeMyTrip launches Multilingual GenAI Trip Planning Assistant” (7 August 2025): https://investors.mmtcdn.com/Press_Release_Gen_AI_Trip_Planning_Assistant_07_08_2025_404cc1872a.pdf
   - Source relevance: Myra was described as a multilingual conversational trip-planning assistant supporting voice and text across travel planning stages.
   - Limitation: company announcement is a primary product source, not an independent impact evaluation.

3. MakeMyTrip investor press release, “MakeMyTrip Deepens AI-First Strategy with OpenAI Collaboration” (18 February 2026): https://investors.mmtcdn.com/Make_My_Trip_Deepens_AI_First_Strategy_with_Open_AI_Collaboration_152a91fca9.pdf
   - Source relevance: MakeMyTrip described integrating OpenAI APIs into app experiences for AI-led discovery and movement toward booking.
   - Limitation: company announcement does not establish performance lift.

4. Booking.com, AI-powered travel planning features: https://news.booking.com/bookingcom-enhances-travel-planning-with-new-ai-powered-features--for-easier-smarter-decisions/
   - Source relevance: Booking.com describes AI Trip Planner, property filtering, property Q&A and review summaries in stated rollout markets.
   - Limitation: availability can vary by market and date; this is used for strategic comparison, not to assert availability in India.

5. Wanderlog, AI Trip Planner and planning product: https://wanderlog.com/trip-planner-ai and https://wanderlog.com/home
   - Source relevance: publicly describes AI assistance, itineraries, map, route optimisation, budget, reservations and collaboration.
   - Limitation: public product pages are not an independent usability or accuracy audit.

6. Expedia, conversational planning and Trip Matching: https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/expedia-launches-conversational-trip-planning-powered-by-chatgpt-to-inspire-members-to-dream-about-travel-in-new-ways/ and https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/now-live-expedia-launches-industry-first-feature-that-turns-reels-on-instagram-into-bookable-travel-itineraries/
   - Source relevance: Expedia described conversational trip planning and a flow that turns travel Reels into bookable itineraries.
   - Limitation: feature availability and exact user experience may vary by market/time.

7. Google Maps, create/share lists of places: https://support.google.com/maps/answer/7280933?hl=en-IN
   - Source relevance: Google Maps supports saved place lists and sharing, making it an indirect substitute for trip organisation.
   - Limitation: saved lists are not a complete budget-aware day planner.

## Evidence policy

- Public product descriptions support competitor comparison but not direct hands-on usability claims.
- RICE confidence values are PM hypotheses, not data-driven probabilities.
- Persona profiles and representative quotes are synthetic proto-persona artefacts, not interview testimony.
- The app has three verified saved sample trips. Only two full Make runs were observed before the latest Persona/acknowledgement changes; one failed the nonempty explanation assertion and one generated a nonempty explanation.
- Eight golden app cases remain unrun, the new Persona-link flow is pending a fresh app event, and the duplicate replay is pending. Do not omit these limitations when sharing the case study.
